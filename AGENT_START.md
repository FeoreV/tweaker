## Contract and safety gates

- Follow the user's `AGENTS.md` constraints, including Win7 SP1 targets, no WMI, backup before mutation, and read-only upstream. No real machine registry, service or filesystem mutation for tests: use mocks or disposable VMs explicitly set up for that purpose.
- The backup requirement includes filesystem objects. Cleanup, repair, external tools and installer operations must not be silently treated as reversible: define what can be captured and restored, fail closed where the invariant cannot be met, and surface gaps to the integrator before enabling actions.
- Persist and verify the pre-mutation record before each mutation, including absent values and exact types/data. A failed backup must prevent mutation. Design recovery for interruption and partial failure; test it.
- Treat handle lifetime, correct closer, pseudo/predefined handles, and thread ownership according to each API's contract. Never assume a pointer-to-integer cast alone transfers ownership or makes a handle safe. Check registry-view requirements by hive and OS; follow the mandatory 64-bit view policy for writes.
- No claim of Win7, Safe Mode, WinPE, Sciter/Preact support, single-file distribution, installer availability, or upstream catalog completeness is accepted without a reproducible check. Verify Sciter runtime/bridge version, licensing and redistribution, architecture, OS support and offline embedding before making it a build dependency. Escalate genuine decision or license blockers instead of inventing an answer.
- Restore point availability and success cannot be assumed. Define and test behavior when unavailable; do not substitute a restore point for a persisted backup.
